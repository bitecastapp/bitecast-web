# Surface: the whole app (sign-up, feed, dish, review, rate, restaurant)

Mode: operate. Phone-first (390 wide), works to desktop. Audience: young people deciding and arguing about what to eat. Product: Letterboxd + Reddit for dishes (ratings, hot takes, Facts/Cap, comments, profiles).

Brand pinned by the owner on 2026-10-21 (brand sheet at `.impeccable/brand/brand-sheet.jpg`): wordmark "nomrank." with a Ketchup dot, tagline "Broadcast your bites.", app icon "b" with broadcast waves. Palette: Studio #17111C, Ketchup #FF4B3A, Mustard #FFC23C, Spotlight #FFF4EA. Owner brief: "jackass like genz yet should not overkill". This replaces the Glasshouse world entirely.

Feed structure chosen by the owner: hot-take poster cards (take as headline, tilted score sticker), with photo thumbnails.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Nomrank is a loud little broadcast booth for food opinions: chunky type, a ketchup-red sticker on every score, Studio-black outlines, and the take itself as the headline. Refuses the polite food-app default (photo grid, white cards, star rows) and refuses meme overload: one tilted thing per card, calm body text.

OWN-WORLD: Spotlight cream ground (#FFF4EA) with Studio (#17111C) ink and 2px Studio outlines; Ketchup (#FF4B3A) for scores, the wordmark dot and the primary action; Mustard (#FFC23C) for highlights, Facts and selection. Urbanist at 800–900 for display (closest open face to the wordmark), 400–600 for UI. Big radii (20–24px cards, pills). Hard offset shadow (3px Studio) only on primary buttons and stickers. Dark cards are Studio with cream text.

STORY: The visitor sees "nomrank." and "Broadcast your bites.", gets in with an email link, watches the signal waves fill as they pick a handle and home, then scrolls hot takes, Facts or Caps them, and rates a dish in seconds.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Login at 390x844: wordmark top-left; a large Studio panel with the broadcast "b" and three waves (first lit); the headline "Rate the dish, not the place." at 44px with "dish" on a Mustard highlight; email field and Ketchup button with a 3px offset shadow at the bottom.

FORM: Brand-pinned (owner-supplied brand sheet), no roll. Signature move: the broadcast waves: sign-up progress lights one wave per step; later, waves mark a hot or live take. Raises carried from earlier rounds: one changing value swaps in place; size carries hierarchy; vast quiet ground with one element owning the field.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
